# Contributing to AgriSathi

Thank you for your interest in improving AgriSathi. Constructive contributions that support accessible, responsible agricultural assistance are welcome.

## Before contributing

Please read:
- [README](README.md)
- [Governance & Participation Policy](GOVERNANCE.md)

These rules apply to all participants, including collaborators, contributors, sponsors, partner organisations, mentors, maintainers, volunteers, service providers, and anyone granted project access.

## Contribution process

1. Discuss major features or architectural changes before doing substantial work.
2. Keep each contribution focused and explain its purpose.
3. Never commit passwords, API keys, access tokens, private keys, personal data, or confidential information.
4. Submit only code, text, images, designs, and other materials you have the right to contribute.
5. Include testing details or reproduction steps where relevant.
6. Be respectful and constructive in reviews and discussions.

## Ownership, authority, and anti-coercion rules

- A contribution does not grant ownership of AgriSathi or control over its direction, repository, accounts, infrastructure, branding, or other project assets.
- Sponsorship, funding, hosting, technical assistance, mentorship, or organisational affiliation does not create ownership or control unless expressly agreed in writing in advance.
- Do not use or threaten to use repository access, credentials, services, funding, infrastructure, or other project resources to compel concessions, ownership or control changes, personal or organisational demands, or unrelated conditions.
- Coercive lockouts, threats, sabotage, deletion, deliberate disruption, retaliation, and unauthorised withholding of project resources are prohibited.
- Material terms cannot be imposed retroactively or unilaterally.
- These rules apply equally regardless of role, title, organisation, seniority, or sponsor status.

## Contribution rights

You are responsible for ensuring you have permission to submit your contribution. Rights in individual contributions are governed by applicable law, the repository licence (if any), and any separate written agreement. Submitting work does not transfer ownership of the overall project.

## Agreement and enforcement

By submitting a contribution or participating in project collaboration, you acknowledge these guidelines and agree to follow them to the extent permitted by applicable law. Violations may result in contributions being declined or reverted, access being suspended or revoked, or collaboration being ended.

These guidelines are project rules, not a substitute for a formal legal contract. Applicable law and valid separate agreements take precedence.
