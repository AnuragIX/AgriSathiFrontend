# AgriSathi Governance & Participation Policy

**Effective from the date this policy is published.**

This policy sets expectations for ownership, decision-making, access, collaboration, and conduct in the AgriSathi project. It applies to every participant: collaborators, contributors, sponsors, partner organisations, mentors, maintainers, volunteers, service providers, and anyone acting on their behalf or using project access.

## 1. Ownership and authority

AgriSathi is an independent project under its designated project owner. Participation, contribution, sponsorship, funding, hosting, mentorship, technical assistance, or organisational affiliation does not by itself grant ownership, administrative control, veto rights, or general decision-making authority.

Any delegated authority must be expressly granted by the project owner, documented where appropriate, and limited to its stated scope. No participant may misrepresent themselves as the owner or an authorised representative.

## 2. Sponsorship and support

Support does not create ownership or control by default. Any sponsor benefits, conditions, access rights, credit arrangements, or other special terms must be clearly documented and accepted by the authorised parties in advance.

## 3. Anti-coercion and anti-extortion

No participant may use, threaten to use, or manipulate access to project resources to force another participant to surrender ownership, control, credit, intellectual property, personal concessions, organisational concessions, or unrelated demands.

Prohibited conduct includes:
- Threatening or carrying out an unauthorised lockout from repositories, accounts, deployments, infrastructure, or other project assets.
- Withholding credentials, code, data, services, funding, or resources to compel concessions that were not agreed in advance.
- Threatening sabotage, deletion, disruption, damage, or deliberate obstruction to pressure another participant.
- Retaliating against someone for disagreeing, refusing new terms, raising a concern, seeking advice, or withdrawing from participation.
- Misrepresenting authority, ownership, agreements, or another participant's consent.
- Imposing material terms, penalties, or obligations retroactively or unilaterally.

These rules apply regardless of title, seniority, organisation, technical role, or sponsor status.

## 4. Access and security

Access is granted for authorised project purposes and should be limited to what a participant reasonably needs. Participants must not misuse credentials or permissions, introduce intentional vulnerabilities, destroy or alter project assets without authorisation, or remove the project owner or authorised maintainers from critical systems without explicit permission or a legitimate and proportionate security reason.

The project owner or authorised maintainers may take reasonable steps to secure accounts, preserve backups, maintain continuity, and suspend or revoke access where necessary. Security measures must not be used as a pretext for coercion or retaliation.

## 5. Contributions and intellectual property

Contributors are responsible for ensuring they have the right to submit their work. Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, a contribution does not transfer ownership of AgriSathi as a whole, its name, branding, repository, accounts, infrastructure, or other project assets, and does not grant governance or administrative authority.

Rights in individual contributions are governed by applicable law, the repository licence (if any), and any valid separate written agreement. Authorised maintainers may review, accept, decline, or revert contributions.

## 6. No unilateral or retroactive changes

No participant may unilaterally impose new material obligations, sponsor conditions, ownership claims, licensing terms, governance arrangements, control changes, or penalties retroactively.

Changes to these arrangements must be communicated clearly and expressly accepted in writing by the authorised parties before taking effect. Silence or continued participation must not be treated as acceptance of undisclosed terms.

## 7. Respectful collaboration and disputes

Participants must act in good faith and avoid harassment, intimidation, threats, coercion, retaliation, and deliberate misrepresentation. Disputes should be documented and addressed through good-faith communication. Raising a legitimate concern or declining an unapproved demand must not result in retaliation.

## 8. Withdrawal and termination

Participants may end their involvement subject to valid prior written obligations and applicable law. Ending support or participation does not automatically transfer ownership or give a withdrawing participant the right to seize, destroy, sabotage, lock out, or withhold unrelated project assets.

## 9. Protective measures

For suspected violations, the project owner or authorised maintainers may reasonably document incidents, preserve relevant records, suspend or revoke access, reject or revert contributions, end collaboration, secure accounts and assets, or seek appropriate advice or remedies. Measures should be proportionate and aimed at protecting the project, its users, participants, and operational continuity.

## 10. Scope and legal note

This policy describes project participation expectations. It does not override applicable law, valid pre-existing written agreements, third-party rights, lawful reporting, or statutory rights. It is not legal advice and is not a substitute for a formal contract. Significant sponsorship, funding, licensing, ownership, intellectual-property, or control arrangements should be covered by a separate written agreement reviewed by qualified legal counsel.

---

**Participation in AgriSathi is based on consent, transparency, and respect.**
